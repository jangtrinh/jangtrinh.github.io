DESIGN:OS hub logos: provenance (corrected 2026-11-01)

What these files are
  Six WebP files: five are 256x256 and design-os-studio.webp is 384x384 (so it is not upscaled on 2x screens
  at its 136px display size). Each is a plain Lanczos downscale plus WebP encode (the Studio one: quality 85,
  method 6; the others' encoder settings were not recorded) of a PNG that exists on Jang's box. Nothing was redrawn, recoloured or generated.
  There are no SVG originals; every source is a raster 3D render, so svgo does not apply.

Usage rights: UNCONFIRMED. Jang has not yet confirmed that he owns these renders or may
  use them as DESIGN:OS brand logos. See "Third-party origin" below. Merge waits for that.

How the files got into the repos' branches
  The 15 PNGs in /workspace/unikorn/logos/logo-small/ (made from /workspace/unikorn/logos/sheet.png,
  copied to the box 2026-10-29) were assigned to repos in the DRAFT
  /workspace/projects/design-os-studio/pm/logo-mapping-2026-10-25.md ("Draft for Jang's approval").
  Commits on "favicon-from-logo" (2026-10-26) then added them as docs/logo.png or site/logo.png.
  Checked with gh and curl on 2026-11-01: the CLI one is on GitHub main (design-os #352, merged). The other five
  are on the pushed branch "favicon-from-logo" of their repos and in open PRs (Studio #1214, Bridge #195,
  SVG Animation #22, Code2Flow #2, 3D Blender #32); none is on main yet. The mapping is still unapproved.
  (An earlier version of this file wrongly said the five were local and unpushed.)

Third-party origin (not resolved)

  "/run/media/lynx/Data/Kerjaan/Nixx Product - Vol.4 (3D Icon)/001 - Nixx Icon 3D/44 - Shape Geometry/Blend/Geometry_6.blend",
